O'Clock

A clock that only shows what you're doing now. When a task's time comes, its slice of the dial opens and the things for it land inside: a bucket and brush for cleaning, a laptop and coffee for work. When nothing is planned, it's just a clock.

Two styles, sticker and hand-drawn, each on the web and as an iPhone home screen widget.
